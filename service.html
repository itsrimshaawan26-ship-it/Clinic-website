KNOW WHEN TO GET CARE

When Should You See a Doctor?

Some symptoms should not be ignored. Early medical consultation can help identify health problems and provide timely care.

01

Frequent Headaches

Consult a doctor if headaches are frequent, severe, or affecting your daily routine.

02

High Blood Pressure

Regular medical consultation is important when blood pressure remains high or difficult to control.

03

Blood Sugar Problems

Get medical guidance if your blood sugar is frequently high or difficult to manage.

04

Digestive Problems

Persistent stomach pain, acidity, or digestive problems may require proper medical evaluation.

05

Joint or Muscle Pain

Ongoing joint or muscle pain should be assessed to understand its possible cause.

06

Thyroid Concerns

Unusual tiredness, weight changes, or other thyroid concerns may need medical consultation.
